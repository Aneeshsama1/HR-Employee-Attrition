# HR Employee Attrition Analysis 👥

## Overview
SQL and Python analysis of 1,470 IBM employees
to identify why staff leave and which groups
are most at risk of attrition.

## Tools Used
- SQL (pandasql)
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
- Kaggle Notebooks

## Key Findings
1. Overall attrition rate: 16.12% — above industry average
2. Sales department highest attrition at 20.63%
3. Under 25s leaving at 39.18% — 4x higher than 35-44 age group
4. Sales Representatives most at risk role at 39.76%
5. Senior roles very stable — Research Director only 2.50%
6. Estimated cost to business: £7M+ annually

## Recommendations
- Invest in retention programmes for under 25s
- Review Sales Rep compensation and career progression
- Investigate why HR department has 19% attrition
- Replicate R&D retention strategies in Sales

## SQL Techniques Used
- CASE WHEN statements
- GROUP BY with aggregate functions
- Subqueries for percentage calculation
- Conditional SUM for attrition counting
